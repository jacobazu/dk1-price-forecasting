{\sffamily\bfseries\Large DK1 day-ahead prices: methods}\par
\href{https://github.com/jacobazu/dk1-price-forecasting}{github.com/jacobazu/dk1-price-forecasting} \textperiodcentered{} October 2026

Forecasts of tomorrow's 24 hourly prices in DK1, made every morning before the noon auction and scored once the prices are out.

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}lL@{}}
\toprule
Inputs & DK1 prices of D$-$1, D$-$2, D$-$3 and D$-$7, neighbour prices of D$-$1, Danish holidays, and weather forecasts for DK1 and Germany made 48 hours ahead. All published before the auction (\texttt{inputs.csv}). \\
Models & BMT-X \citep{kapetanios2026}, LEAR-X \citep{lago2021}, XGBoost-X \citep{chen2016} and their average, refitted daily on two years, on asinh-transformed prices \citep{uniejewski2018}. Benchmark: the weekly naive. \\
Scoring & MAE and rMAE; Diebold--Mariano tests with a Newey--West variance \citep{diebold1995,newey1987}; Giacomini--White \citep{giacomini2006}; model confidence set \citep{hansen2011}; Mincer--Zarnowitz \citep{mincer1969}. \\
Ranges & Quantiles of past errors with adaptive conformal inference \citep{gibbs2021}; pinball loss, coverage tests, and the Brier score for negative prices. \\
Battery & A 2\,MWh battery planned on the forecast with a mixed-integer linear program; revenue at risk by EWMA-filtered historical simulation \citep{hull1998}, tested with \citet{kupiec1995}, \citet{christoffersen1998} and \citet{acerbi2014}, and compared with the FZ0 loss \citep{patton2019}. \\
Rules & Only data published before the auction, and the backtest uses the same inputs. Test rules are written before the test. Changes found in the backtest run live as challengers first. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
